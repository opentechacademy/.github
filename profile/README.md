# Open Tech Academy

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Open Tech Academy**! We are a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to making technology education accessible, hands-on, and community-driven. 

Our mission is to break down the barriers to tech literacy by providing open-source resources, curriculum, and interactive hardware workshops for students and creators everywhere.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

We believe the best way to learn technology is by building it. Open Tech Academy focuses on:
* **Open-Source Curriculum:** Developing free, high-quality learning tracks for programming, robotics, and hardware engineering.
* **Hands-On Workshops:** Hosting community events—like our "Build to Own" computer workshops—where participants get direct experience with physical hardware.
* **Community Innovation:** Creating a space where students and tech enthusiasts can collaborate on real-world projects and open-source software.
